Four tables collect the transparency data released by service providers and
\V{NCMEC} regarding CyberTipline reports and attached pieces, aggregated by
service provider and year and, where possible, accounting for both sources. The
parenthesized material after the organization name identifies the frequency of
data disclosures as Q/H/Y for every 4/6/12 months and whether the provider
proactively scans pictures (\ImageCheck) or videos (\VideoCheck). Pinterest is
marked ``Q/H'' because the firm releases quarterly aggregates every six months.

The $\Delta\%$ column shows the percentage difference between the two report
counts in terms of the mean. Identical entries are marked as $\equiv$. Some of
the percentage of all reports are too small to fit into the alloted three-digit
precision and hence are marked as $\qtiny$. Since years in the first column and
total report counts in the last column are repeated for every service provider,
the are presented in gray, thus making the table look less busy. Every provider
is alloted the same space, leaving entries with no data blank, to avoid biasing
the presentation. Finally, poviders are sorted in descending order of report
counts for 2024.
